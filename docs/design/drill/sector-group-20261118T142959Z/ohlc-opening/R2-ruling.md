## Arbiter disposition

- **NVDA.** The observed earnings beat directly affects the issuer. A qualitative after-hours rise supports early relief. Zealot raised its central D+3 close from $157 to $160 while reducing its durable-upside weight by five percentage points. The size of the initial move and funding quality remain estimates.
- **AMD and TSM.** Accelerator spending can favor the incumbent and bypass AMD. A shift among designers could still use the same foundry. This comparison identifies different exposures to one event. Changes in AMD share or TSM wafer volumes remain unverified. Both AMD trees stood; Zealot reduced the TSM upside weight by four points.
- **AVGO.** Greater spending on Nvidia systems can lift network demand, while Broadcom's custom-chip case depends on separate customer programs. Its relative strength supports treating the issuer independently. Zealot reduced the joint-expansion probability from 35% to 30%. No bookings evidence establishes the exact weight.
- **MU.** The shipping Oxide product and HBM demand support a plausible volume channel, while NAND estimates can move independently. Margin and allocation magnitudes remain open. Zealot trimmed three points from the HBM-led branch. Reaper retained its mild downside skew.

The funding and allocation breakdown remains unknown. The arbiter accepts the distinctions among business exposures. Missing disclosure can justify removing an earlier unsupported premium; it supplies no measured adverse shift, and does not determine whether a three-, four- or five-point revision is correct.
