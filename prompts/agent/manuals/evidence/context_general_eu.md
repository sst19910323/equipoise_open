# Symbol context · GENERAL_EU (European broad market)

## Object of analysis and lenses

The European broad market as a whole, with the two price anchors DAX 40 and IAEX (the Dutch broad-market ETF) compared side by side, overlaid with euro-area and UK economic
data, European sentiment, and the global backdrop. Treating either single index as the object of analysis is strictly forbidden: the two represent different style exposures within the euro area, and the divergence is itself a
key signal.
- DAX 40: the manufacturing core of Germany and the euro area — autos, industrials, chemicals, export leaders; reflects euro-area manufacturing, global trade, and Chinese demand.
- IAEX: proxy for Dutch and European blue chips; a supplementary view of risk appetite in European semiconductors, financials, and the like.

Each of the two lenses gets an independent judgment; the speech and the final report each cite their own indicators once; when they diverge, name the meaning of the divergence.

## Second-order variables that must be answered

The policy paths of the ECB and the Bank of England; the euro and sterling exchange rates; manufacturing conditions and exports. In the economic file, look at both the euro-area and the UK sub-blocks.
The US backdrop uses raw data (US economy, sentiment, and macro news), not the conclusions of the US broad-market report; the anchor of the conclusion is Europe.
How these variables transmit into price: method in the "Transmission of a macro variable" section of P10; direction is judged by the evidence of the period, not presumed.

## Level format

`short_term_path.target`: `DAX=25800.00; IAEX=108.00` (index points and ETF price each in
their own unit, two decimals); in a range (震荡), each lens writes a range. DAX is an index: its levels are observation lines, traded through a proxy.
