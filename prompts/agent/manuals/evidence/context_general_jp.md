# Symbol context · GENERAL_JP (Japanese broad market)

## Object of analysis and lenses

The Japanese broad market as a whole, with the two price anchors Nikkei 225 and TOPIX compared side by side, overlaid with Japanese economic data and sentiment. Treating either single index as the
object of analysis is forbidden: the two weight the market differently, and their divergence is itself a key signal.
- N225: price-weighted, dominated by a few high-priced names (tech, retail, precision); the yen-sensitive export leaders and the index's heavyweights move it.
- TOPX (TOPIX): market-cap weighted across the whole Prime market — banks, autos, trading houses, domestic demand; the broader read of Japanese corporate earnings and domestic conditions.

Each of the two lenses gets an independent judgment; the speech and the final report each cite their own indicators once; when they diverge, name the meaning of the divergence. Never look at index points
alone — the second-order variables below must be overlaid.

## Second-order variables that must be answered

- Dollar-yen: a second-order variable that must be answered. The direction of transmission from the exchange rate to the export leaders and to the index is judged by the evidence of the period, with no presumed direction; give the currently
  key exchange-rate levels.
- Bank of Japan policy: rate-hike expectations, yield-curve control, FX intervention.
- The Tankan survey and export data: business conditions and actual export performance.
- Sector structure: who inside the index is leading the rise and leading the fall (autos, semiconductors, banks, consumer); do not look only at index points in the aggregate.
- The US backdrop uses raw data, not the conclusions of the US broad-market report; the anchor of the conclusion is Japan.

How these variables transmit into price: method in the "Transmission of a macro variable" section of P10.

## Level format

`short_term_path.target`: `N225=39500.00; TOPX=2750.00` (index points, two decimals, each lens in its own unit); in a range (震荡), each lens writes a range. Both are indices: their levels are
observation lines, traded through a proxy. The exchange rate does not enter the operation level; discuss it in the speech and the final report.
