# Symbol context · ETF

## Object of analysis and lenses

The ETF itself, read within its benchmark. Explicitly judge its strength and divergence relative to the benchmark:
- Sector ETF: focus on explaining the direction of the sector's rotation relative to the broad market;
- Broad-market, thematic, or commodity ETF: explain its attractiveness relative to vehicles of the same kind; whom it is compared against is set by the benchmark in the configuration.

## Second-order variables that must be answered

- Broad-market regime: risk appetite on or off; even the best sector cannot outrun passive de-risking when risk is off; risk control layers market-level triggers on top. Whether a macro news item
  concerns this ETF at all: method in the "Transmission of a macro variable" section of P10.
- Sector and factor rotation: flows between groups (for example megacap technology versus cyclicals, financials or defensives) in the frozen news and the relative price action, read in both directions.
- The valuation bridge where it is meaningful for the fund: a dated earnings yield against the same-date 10-year yield, or the fund's own rate sensitivity (for financials, the curve slope on net interest income), with basis and arithmetic; a valuation input, not a timing rule.
- Constituent concentration: to what extent a few heavily weighted stocks determine its path.

## Level format

`operation_target` and `short_term_path.target` take a single numeric level.
